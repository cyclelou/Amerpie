# Sara Blakely

[[+Quotes MOC|Quotes]]

quote:: My dad would actually encourage me to fail growing up. I would come home from school and he would say to my brother and I: 'So what'd you guys fail at this week?' And if we didn't have something, he would actually be disappointed. I didn't realize it at the time, but he was just changing my definition of failure. My definition of failure became not about the outcome, but about not trying.
