# Simon Sinek

[[+Quotes MOC|Quotes]]

quote:: There is no decision that we can make that doesn't come with some sort of balance or sacrifice.

quote:: People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it and what you do simply proves what you believe. In fact, people will do the things that prove what they believe. The reason that person bought the iPhone in the first six hours, stood in line for six hours, was because of what they believed about the world, and how they wanted everybody to see them: they were first. People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it.
